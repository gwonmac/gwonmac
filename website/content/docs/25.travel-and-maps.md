These Features help you get to a place, see what is left to do there, and find it on the map. Each one has its own page:

- [Travel]($docs.travel): go to an outpost, a friend's outpost or your Guild Hall.
- [Progress]($docs.travel#progress): see what a title goal still needs, and travel to your quest.
- [Maps]($docs.cartography): the exploration grid, walkable terrain, a walking route, Compass ranges and the elite skill planner.

Turn each one on in the launcher's Features page. They are off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}.
