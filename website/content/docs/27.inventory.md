Two Features save trips and clicks when you manage items. Each one has its own page:

- [Xunlai Storage]($docs.xunlai-storage): open your storage chest from a supported outpost.
- [Quick Item Move]($docs.quick-item-move): move whole stacks in one click through storage or a trade window.

Turn each one on in the launcher's Features page. They are off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}.
