These Features add numbers and prompts to the game screen. They only show information, apart from Resign, which asks before it acts. Every one works in PvE and is off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}. Each one has its own page:

- [Effect Timers]($docs.effect-timers): the time left on your Effects icons.
- [Skill Cooldowns]($docs.skill-cooldowns): recharge numbers on your skill bar.
- [Skill Key Labels]($docs.skill-key-labels): your own key names on the eight skill slots.
- [Target Distance]($docs.target-readout): how far away your target is.
- [Reminders]($docs.reminders): a chat line before a con, pcon or alcohol runs out.
- [Alcohol Timer]($docs.alcohol-timer): a countdown while you are drunk.
- [Resign]($docs.resign): asks before it sends `/resign`.
