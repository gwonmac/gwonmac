Effect Timers shows the exact time left on the icons in your Effects panel. Above 99 seconds it shows minutes, such as `2m`. Below that it shows seconds, and tenths of a second in the last 3 seconds.

### Use it
Turn it on. The numbers show on your own effects at once. Effects with no end, or a very long one, show no number.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Effect Timers]{to="features/tool:effect-timers"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** always visible once on. It has no window or shortcut.

> It shows your own effects only, in PvE. It shows nothing for party members or enemies.
