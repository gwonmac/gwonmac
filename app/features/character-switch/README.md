Switch Character shows your characters as cards. Choose one, and the game signs in to it without a logout. It also works from the character selection screen.

### Use it
1. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>, or search `char` in the [Hub]($docs.hub).
2. Type a name or a primary profession.
3. Press Enter, click **Switch to …**, or double-click a card.

Each card shows the profession pair, such as `Mo/Me`, and marks PvP characters. The number keys 1 to 9 and 0 select one of the first ten characters but never switch.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Switch Character]{to="features/tool:character-switch"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>, **View → Switch Character**, or search `char` in the Hub.
- **Settings:** :setting[Show profession]{to="features/characterSwitchProfession"}, :setting[Show level]{to="features/characterSwitchLevel"}, :setting[Show location]{to="features/characterSwitchLocation"}.
- **Limits:** names and search text are not saved.

> In an explorable area it asks “Leave this area and switch to …?” first. While a map loads or in active PvP, it does not switch.
