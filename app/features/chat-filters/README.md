Chat Filters hides the system notices you choose before they reach your chat log.

### Use it
1. Turn on Chat Filters.
2. Choose what to hide. Each option hides one kind of message when it is on: :setting[Hide other party members' item drops]{to="features/chatFilterAllyDrops"}, :setting[Hide Hall of Heroes winner announcements]{to="features/chatFilterHallOfHeroes"}, :setting[Hide player title achievements]{to="features/chatFilterTitleAchievements"}.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Chat Filters]{to="features/tool:chat-filters"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}.

> It works outside PvP.
