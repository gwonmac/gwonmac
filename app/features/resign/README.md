Resign asks before it sends `/resign`. A dialog shows first, so a key press by mistake cannot end your mission.

### Use it
1. Give Resign a shortcut on its Feature page, choose **View → Resign…**, or search `resign` in the [Hub]($docs.hub).
2. Press Enter to confirm, or Escape to cancel.

The dialog accepts Enter only after a short moment, so the key press that opened it cannot also confirm it.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Resign]{to="features/tool:resign"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** its shortcut, which is unassigned by default, **View → Resign…**, or the Hub.

> It works in PvE only, in an outpost or explorable area. Finish or clear your chat draft first.
