The Exploration grid shows the cells you can still explore on the Mission Map and World Map, so you can finish a Cartographer title without guessing. Hollow amber markers are estimates. Solid orange markers are confirmed for the map you are in.

Hold Shift over a Mission Map cell to see the 3×3 area standing there reveals. Hold Option and Shift for the 7×7 Bird’s Eye area.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Exploration grid]{to="features/tool:maps"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** the grid shows on the maps once it is on. You can assign a shortcut to **Exploration grid** on its Feature page; it starts unassigned. In the [Hub]($docs.hub), the **Exploration grid** row opens these settings.
- **Settings:** :setting[Markers on Compass]{to="features/cartographyCompassGridEnabled"} adds nearby unexplored cells to the Compass. :setting[Reveal range]{to="features/cartographyRevealMode"} (Off, Normal · 3×3, Bird’s Eye · 7×7) shows on the Compass the area your position reveals. :setting[Grid opacity]{to="features/cartographyGridOpacity"} sets how strongly the grid shows.
- **Map look:** the map style, the terrain border and the Compass buttons are in Settings › Appearance, because every map Feature shares them. See [Appearance]($docs.appearance#maps-and-compass).
- **Limits:** supported PvE maps only. The grid covers Pre-Searing, Tyria, Cantha and Elona. In dungeons, underground maps, the Battle Isles and the Realm of Torment, the grid hides. It returns after you travel to a fully supported area.
