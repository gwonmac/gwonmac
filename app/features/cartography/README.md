Maps draws guidance on the game’s own maps: an exploration grid, walkable terrain, a walking route and range rings on the Compass. Each layer has its own switch.

### Walking route
1. Right-click the Mission Map or World Map to set a point.
2. Follow the line on the Compass.
3. Right-click the point again to remove it.

Without a point of your own, the line leads to your active quest.

### Exploration grid
The grid shows the cells you can still explore on the Mission Map and World Map. Hold Shift over a cell to see what standing there reveals.

> The route to your quest is solid. The route to your own point is dashed.
