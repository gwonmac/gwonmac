## Unreleased

- Maps is now the Exploration grid. The walking route, Compass ranges and the Elite Skill Planner are Features of their own, each with its own switch. If you had Maps off, all four stay off.

- Every Maps setting is now on its Feature page: in the launcher's Features tab, and in the game under Hub › Settings › Features.
