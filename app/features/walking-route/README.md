The Walking route draws a yellow line from you to a point you set, on the Compass, Mission Map and World Map, so you can walk to a place without checking the map at every turn.

### Use it
1. Right-click the Mission Map or World Map to set a point.
2. Follow the line on the Compass.
3. Right-click the point again to remove it.

The line follows walkable ground and updates when you leave it. When no walkable way reaches the point, the line ends on the nearest ground and a dotted line shows the rest. The point stays when you travel to another area of the same continent, so you can set it on the World Map and follow it across several areas. It is removed when you reach another continent or close the game. In another area, the line leads to the ground nearest the point, which is not always the exit you need.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Walking route]{to="features/tool:walking-route"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. It is on by default; nothing shows until you set a point.
- **Open:** you can assign a shortcut to **Walking route** and **Walkable terrain** on its Feature page; both start unassigned. In the [Hub]($docs.hub), the **Walking route** row opens these settings.
- **Settings:** :setting[Walkable terrain]{to="features/cartographyOverlayEnabled"} shades the areas you cannot walk on, on the Compass and Mission Map. :setting[Walkable terrain opacity]{to="features/cartographyWalkabilityOpacity"} sets how strongly it shows. The line's colors follow the map style on the [Exploration grid]($docs.cartography) page.
- **Limits:** supported PvE maps only.
