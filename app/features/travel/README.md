Travel finds an outpost by the start of its name (`kam`, `jewel istan`), by an official short name (`ha`, `eotn`), or by a phrase you saved. Press Enter to go there. It also lists recent places and your favourites.

### Use it
1. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>, or type a place name in the [Hub]($docs.hub).
2. Type the start of a name, such as `kam`.
3. Press Enter.

Places your character has not unlocked stay in the list with the reason, and cannot be chosen. So does the outpost you are in.

### Favourites and your own phrases
In Travel, **Customize** saves your own search phrases (up to 64, 1 to 40 characters each) and favourites. With an empty search, the number keys 1 to 9 select a favourite. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd> save the selected place under that number.

### Friends and Guild Hall
Search a friend’s alias or character name to see where they are. **Travel to outpost** takes you to their outpost in any district. It does not join their exact instance. A friend who is offline, or whose location is out of date, cannot be travelled to. To invite a friend after the trip, see **Travel and invite** under [Chat and trade]($docs.chat-and-trade#invite-to-party).

Search `guild hall` or `gh` to go to your Guild Hall. In the hall, the same row reads **Leave Guild Hall** and returns you to the outpost you came from. Travel shows **No Guild Hall** when your character has none.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Travel]{to="features/tool:quick-travel"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. Progress, below, follows this switch.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>, **View → Travel**, the chat command `/tp`, or search for a place name in the Hub. You can change the shortcut on its Feature page.
- **Limits:** PvE only. A trip that the game delays, for one of its own prompts or a party countdown, is not reported as a failure: Travel says that the trip has not started yet and follows it when it does.

> In an explorable area, every trip first asks “Leave this area and travel to …?”. **Stay here** is selected, so Enter stays.
