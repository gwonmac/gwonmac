Travel finds an outpost by the start of its name, by an official short name such as **ha**, or by a phrase you saved. Press Enter to go there.

### Use it
1. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>, or type a place name in the Hub.
2. Type the start of a name, such as **kam**.
3. Press Enter.

You can also travel to a friend’s outpost or your Guild Hall. With an empty search, the number keys 1 to 9 pick your favourites.

> In an explorable area, every trip asks first. Places your character has not unlocked show why and cannot be chosen.
