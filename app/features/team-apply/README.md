Team Apply loads a saved team from the [Build Library]($docs.build-library) onto your party: the heroes in their order, with their skills and attributes.

### Use it
1. Open the Build Library with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>.
2. Open a saved team.
3. Choose to apply it in an outpost.

Each step must show in the game before the next one starts. If one step does not land, Team Apply stops and tells you, so no hero keeps an old bar without you knowing.

> It follows the Build Library switch. It works in PvE only.
