Quick Item Move moves a whole item stack with one click while storage or a trade window is open.

### Use it
1. Open Xunlai Storage or a trade window.
2. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click an item to move the whole stack.
3. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click an item to choose the amount.

From your inventory, the item goes into your trade offer when a trade is open, and into storage when it is not. From storage, it goes to your inventory. The other player’s offer never changes.

> It does not work during PvP play.
