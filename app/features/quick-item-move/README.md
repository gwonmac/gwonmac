Quick Item Move moves a whole item stack with one click while storage or a trade window is open.

### Use it
1. Open [Xunlai Storage]($docs.xunlai-storage) or a trade window.
2. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click an item to move the whole stack.
3. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click an item to choose the amount in Guild Wars’ own quantity box.

Where the stack goes depends on what is open:

- In your inventory with a trade open, the item goes into your trade offer. Otherwise it goes into storage.
- In storage, the item goes to your inventory.
- On your own trade offer, a click takes that offer back.
- The other player’s offer is never changed.

A stack fills matching stacks first, then empty slots. If there is no room, the rest stays where it was.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Quick Item Move]{to="tools/tool:quick-item-move"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="tools/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Open:** it has no window. Open Xunlai Storage or a trade window, then <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click an item.

> It works only with an open storage or trade window, and not during PvP play.
