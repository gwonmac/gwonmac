Alcohol Timer shows a small beer icon and a countdown while you are drunk. It turns amber in the last 15 seconds and disappears at zero. It is hidden when you are sober.

### Use it
1. Turn it on.
2. Drink.
3. Drag the timer in a game window to move it.

The timer sits near the top-left of the game view and keeps its distance from the nearest corner when you resize the window. To place it while you are sober, choose **Show to place** in :setting[Alcohol Timer position]{to="features/alcoholTimerPosition"}, drag it, then select its lock. **Put back** returns it to its first place.

If you turn the timer on while already drunk, it can stay hidden until your next drink. Nothing is saved between sessions.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Alcohol Timer]{to="features/tool:alcohol-timer"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}. It is off by default.
- **Limits:** PvE only.

> The time is an estimate: one minute per alcohol level.
