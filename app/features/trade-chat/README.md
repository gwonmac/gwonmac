Trade Chat is a window to browse recent trade messages from Kamadan and Pre-Searing. It only reads; you post your own offers in Guild Wars, as before.

### Use it
1. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, or type `trade` and an item in the [Hub]($docs.hub).
2. Search for an item or a character name, and filter by **All**, **Selling** or **Buying**.
3. Select an offer to read it in full, copy the character name or the offer, or whisper the seller.

**Saved** keeps the offers you starred and the players you follow. **Trader prices** shows Kamadan NPC trader quotes with a short price history.

Drag the window by its title bar and resize it from its corner; every game window keeps that place and size. In the launcher's Features tab, **Put back** under **Trade window position** returns it to its default place.

### Details
- **Turn on:** :setting[Trade Chat]{to="features/tool:trade-chat"}, off while you play the :setting[plain game]{to="features/gwonmacTools"}.
- **Open:** <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, **View → Trade Chat**, the chat command `/trade`, or `trade` and an item in the Hub.
- **Limits:** works outside PvP. When the feed drops, it shows “Waiting for Kamadan to reconnect” with a **Try again** button. **Whisper** in an offer needs [Whispers]($docs.whispers) on.

> Trade Chat needs an internet connection. It reads a public trade feed from kamadan.gwtoolbox.com and ascalon.gwtoolbox.com.
