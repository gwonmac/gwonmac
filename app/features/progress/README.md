Progress shows what a title goal still needs for your current character: missions, hard mode missions, vanquishes and outposts. Each missing place is one trip away.

### Use it
1. Search **progress** in the Hub, or a goal word such as **vq**, **carto** or **hm**.
2. Pick a goal.
3. Press Enter on a place to travel there.

When a quest is selected in your quest log, the Hub also offers **Travel to quest**.

> Progress follows the Travel switch. It works in PvE only, with a character.
