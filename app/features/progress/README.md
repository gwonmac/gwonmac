Progress shows what a title goal still needs for your current character: missions, hard mode missions, vanquishes and outposts. Each missing place is one trip away.

### Use it
1. Search `progress` in the [Hub]($docs.hub), or a goal word: `vq`, `guardian`, `protector`, `carto`, `hm`, `outposts`.
2. Pick a goal.
3. Press Enter on a place to travel there.

A mission takes you to its outpost. An area or a locked mission goes to the nearest unlocked outpost on the same world map, and the button says “(nearest)”.

Goals depend on your campaign. Prophecies, Factions and Nightfall list the Protector and Guardian titles, Vanquisher, Cartographer and Outposts. Eye of the North lists Missions, Hard mode missions, Vanquishes and Outposts. Cartographer shows a percentage per continent only, because the game does not say which areas are missing.

### Travel to quest
When a quest is selected in your quest log, the Hub’s start page offers **Travel to quest** first. Press Enter to go to the outpost the quest leads to. When the quest leads into an explorable area, or its outpost is not unlocked yet, the trip goes to the nearest unlocked outpost, and the row says which one.

The start page leaves the row out when no unlocked outpost is near the quest or you are already there; search `quest` to see why. It reads only which map the quest leads to, not the quest’s name or text.

### Details
- **Turn on:** Progress has no switch of its own. It follows :setting[Travel]{to="tools/tool:quick-travel"}.
- **Open:** search `progress`, a goal word or `quest` in the Hub.
- **Limits:** at loading screens, in PvP and at character selection it shows a waiting line. A goal whose record cannot be read is left out instead of showing 0%. The goal you last picked is not saved between sessions.

> Progress works in PvE only, with a character.
